Local brain notes through 2.63.0 add a focusing-illusion check: overweighting a single salient factor (income, status, one metric, one risk or benefit) as if it dominated overall value, happiness or decision quality, while ignoring the broader set of factors, adaptation and trade-offs, is a hypothesis, not evidence. Local brain notes through 2.62.0 add an IKEA-effect check: placing disproportionately high value on a product, plan or outcome solely because one partially created, assembled or invested personal effort into it, without independent valuation or market comparison, is a hypothesis, not evidence. Local brain notes through 2.61.0 fix the coordinator confidence ceiling so both Russian «возможный сигнал» and English «possible signal» markers count, and tell the coordinator that agreeing wording is not evidence. Discarding evidence because it contradicts a preferred side is a myside-bias hypothesis, not proof. A probability quoted to hundredths without a range is a false-precision hypothesis, not a sharper forecast. Local brain notes through 2.60.0 add an endowment-effect check: treating currently owned, held or status-quo assets/options as intrinsically more valuable than equivalent alternatives solely because they are already "mine", without willingness-to-pay / willingness-to-accept calibration or market comparison, is a hypothesis, not evidence. Local brain notes through 2.59.0 add an automation-bias check: accepting a recommendation solely because it came from an AI, algorithm or automated system, while dismissing contradictory independent evidence without acknowledging model limitations, is a hypothesis, not evidence. ...
